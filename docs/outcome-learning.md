# Outcome Learning V1

A business outcome is a human-recorded fact linked to a completed tenant-owned Decision and Prospect. Allowed types are meeting booked, opportunity advanced, won, lost, not interested, bad fit, no response, and other. A send, approval, or SLA resolution never creates an outcome. Corrections append a new revision; the latest revision is effective. Each revision creates one idempotent Prospect Memory event in the same transaction. Decision Replay displays the outcome after the original Decision evidence.

Learning runs only when the user selects **Refresh learnings**. It reads at most 1,000 recent outcome revisions per tenant and suppresses suggestions if that cap is exceeded. V1 compares human-recorded positive outcomes (meeting booked, opportunity advanced, won) between Decisions with a recorded SLA breach and Decisions with a resolved SLA and no recorded breach. `OTHER`, missing SLA evidence, and ambiguous timing are excluded from the comparison. The window is 90 days; a suggestion requires at least 10 eligible outcomes overall, 5 in each compared group, and a gap of at least 20 percentage points. These are product thresholds, not statistical significance.

Suggestions state counts, rates, date range, and caveats using association language, never causation. A fingerprint of the compared outcome revisions and their groups prevents repeats from unchanged evidence. An explicit refresh archives an open suggestion if its evidence no longer qualifies. A human may accept a suggestion **for review** or reject it; actor, time, and note are recorded. Neither action edits Constitution, Playbook, Brain, ICP, automation, risk weights, or SLA policy. Aggregation and review make zero AI calls and create no AI usage records.
