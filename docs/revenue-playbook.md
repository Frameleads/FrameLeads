# Revenue Playbook

The Revenue Playbook stores tenant-owned preferences for handling repeatable sales situations. It is guidance for future decision systems, not an enforced Sales Constitution, reply classifier, automatic sender, or prospect timeline. Existing `GovernanceRule` behavior remains unchanged; its metrics are separate. No starter rules are inserted, so generic advice is never presented as a tenant's own policy.

`RevenuePlaybook` is unique per user and holds the current revision. `RevenuePlaybookRule` holds a bounded scenario, objective, handling guidance, preferred next action, optional response principles and CTA guidance, optional qualification/direction applicability, priority, enabled/archive state, source and entry revision. The scenario enum covers common pricing, integration, objection, meeting, timing, proof and other situations plus `CUSTOM`. Current creation uses `USER_ENTERED`; `SYSTEM_DEFAULT` is reserved for a future explicitly sourced default, with a stable nullable source key. Similar user-authored rules may coexist.

The central service exposes `getOrCreateRevenuePlaybook`, `listPlaybookRules`, `createPlaybookRule`, `updatePlaybookRule`, `archivePlaybookRule`, `getPlaybookRevision` and `getPlaybookContext`. Writes run in serializable tenant-scoped transactions. Creating/enabling/changing/disabling/archiving effective active guidance increments the root revision; reads, identical edits and edits to disabled guidance do not. No absolute rule is enforced by these functions.

`getPlaybookContext({ userId, purpose, scenarios, qualificationStatus, direction, maxRules, maxCharacters })` selects enabled, unarchived matching rules. Explicit scenarios rank before purpose-relevant scenarios and general guidance. Priority, condition specificity and recency break ties. It examines at most 100 candidates, returns at most 10 rules, clips each rendered field and caps text at 5,000 characters. Future callers may compose it with `getBrainContext` for company facts and `getProspectMemoryContext` for prospect history. Those stores remain separate. Scenario detection belongs to future Inbox Triage; hard boundaries belong to Sales Constitution; execution controls belong to Automation Modes.

Authenticated `/dashboard/playbook` and `/api/playbook` routes provide minimal manual rule management. Client data cannot supply `userId`, source or raw Prisma filters. No AI provider, embedding, external call or fake AI usage event is used. Migration `20260928140000_revenue_playbook` is additive and was not deployed during implementation.
